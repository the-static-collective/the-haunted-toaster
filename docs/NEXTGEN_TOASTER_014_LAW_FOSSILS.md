# NEXTGEN-TOASTER-014 — LAW FOSSILS

Status: experimental descendant of NEXTGEN-TOASTER-013W Weirdness Compiler.

## Thesis

A future material may inherit evidence about which world assumptions were explicitly relaxed when an ancestor's pixels were rendered.

That evidence is provenance, not an active law.

    WeirdnessCompilation
          ↓
    compiled PerformanceProgram
          ↓
    verified whole-render frame graph
          ↓
    LawFossilV0 + WorldLawRenderCauseV0
          ↓
    rendered-history capsule
          ↓
    exact-byte video re-entry
          ↓
    Video Digestion descendants inherit bounded fossil refs

## Causal distinction

A LawFossil by itself proves that a weirdness compilation existed. It does not prove those relaxed assumptions caused pixels.

WorldLawRenderCauseV0 closes the causal chain by requiring:

- the exact LawFossil;
- the exact WeirdnessCompilation;
- the exact compiled program hash;
- a valid whole-render receipt;
- the exact renderer identity;
- the exact whole-render receipt hash;
- the exact frameGraphHash.

The compiled program hash must agree across the fossil, compilation, and whole-render receipt.

    WEIRDNESS WAS PRESENT != WEIRDNESS CAUSED PIXELS

Only a matching causal witness may propagate as inherited law history.

## Inherited ref

Video descendants receive only a bounded provenance ref:

    lawFossilRef
      lawFossilHash
      weirdnessCompilationHash
      sourceProgramHash
      compiledProgramHash
      axes[{ axisId, amount }]

    worldLawCauseRef
      causeHash
      renderer
      wholeRenderReceiptHash
      frameGraphHash

They do not inherit executable Weirdness bindings or active transforms.

    LAW FOSSIL != ACTIVE LAW
    INHERITED WEIRDNESS != REACTIVATED WEIRDNESS
    RELAXED LAW CAUSE != FUTURE LAW
