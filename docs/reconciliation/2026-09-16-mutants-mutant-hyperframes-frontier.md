## HyperFrames proof gates

Before calling the first adapter kosher, require:

1. **SPECIMEN PARITY** — identical `hf0_` specimen + trace produces identical canonical adapter model/source.
2. **NO BACKFLOW** — changing renderer-only choices cannot change `hf0_` identity or accepted song/phrase authority.
3. **FINITE TIMELINE** — generated composition has explicit bounded duration and no ambient time/randomness.
4. **LINT** — HyperFrames lint passes.
5. **INSPECT** — HyperFrames inspection reports the expected composition/timeline/asset surface.
6. **RENDER** — one complete living-receipt descendant renders successfully.
7. **REPLAY RECEIPT** — projection receipt records specimen id, trace id/hash, adapter policy/version, renderer version/environment, and output identity.
8. **FIELD WITNESS** — human can distinguish intended HyperFood mechanics (at minimum FRAME-EAT-FRAME recursion and PULSE response) in the projection.

Only after these gates should richer HyperFood organisms or HyperFrames-specific visual vocabulary be admitted.
