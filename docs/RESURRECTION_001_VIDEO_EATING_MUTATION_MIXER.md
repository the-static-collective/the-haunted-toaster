
The new contract is:

- schema: `haunted-toaster/foreign-material-mutation-family/v1`
- policy: `resurrection-video-eating-v1`
- all descendants share the same admitted specimen identity, source hash, and clip-analysis hash;
- each descendant has a distinct plan hash;
- family identity follows source bytes and canonical plans, not local path or filename;
- the texture descendant remains byte-for-byte the same plan produced by the established foreign-material control path;
- topology and motion descendants do not carry literal source pixels into the native image.

The existing `video-digestion-v1` two-descendant family is intentionally unchanged. Historical proof remains replayable.
