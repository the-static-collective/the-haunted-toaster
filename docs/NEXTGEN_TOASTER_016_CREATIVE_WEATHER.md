## CreativeWeatherV0

Schema: static-collective/creative-weather/v0

Policy: bounded-listening-testimony-weather/v0

Authority: testimony-derived-only

Weather is sparse.

It is derived only for explicitly requested exact frames rather than materializing a giant per-frame analytics surface.

Each sample contains the same five canonical channels.

### section-boundary

Linear proximity to exact section-boundary witnesses inside the configured frame window.

### lyric-presence

Active lyric-cue testimony at the exact frame. Strength is bounded by the cue confidence when available.

### human-anchor

Linear proximity to explicit human-authoritative lyric anchors.

### landmark

Linear proximity to admitted existing song landmarks.

### testimony-density

Bounded count of all ListeningField witnesses whose spans fall within the local weather window.

Density saturates at eight witnesses.

These channels describe testimony geometry, not song meaning.

    CHANNEL STRENGTH != IMPORTANCE
    MISSING WITNESS != ZERO SONG FEATURE

## Explicit weather bindings

Transition Energy does not react to weather automatically.

A candidate must explicitly declare a weather binding:

    bindingId
    channelId
    direction = lower | raise
    maxDelta <= 0.25

Example:

    section boundary testimony
        ↓ explicit binding
    transition from steady → turn
        ↓
    lower energy by at most 0.20

At another frame where section-edge strength is weaker, the same binding contributes less.

No binding means zero weather contribution.

    TESTIMONY != FORCE
    WEATHER BINDING != ACCEPTANCE

## Raising energy

Weather may also make a crossing harder.

Example:

    lyric-presence
        ↓ explicit raise binding
    visible → vanish

could become more expensive during an active lyric cue.

This is still not a recommendation to keep the material visible.

## Recurrence is deliberately dormant

The 012A ListeningField port contains section, lyric, human-anchor, and existing-landmark witnesses.

It does not yet contain a causal recurrence-region lane.

Therefore 016 does not infer recurrence from labels, section names, repeated lyrics, or material behavior.

    NO RECURRENCE WITNESS
        →
    NO RECURRENCE WEATHER

A later slice may add recurrence testimony with its own provenance and validation.

## Canonicality

CreativeWeather sample frames are unique and ascending.

Channels have one fixed order.

Witness IDs are unique and sorted.

Candidate weather bindings are canonicalized by channel.

One candidate may bind to a channel at most once.

Therefore array order cannot become hidden creative preference.
