## RecurrenceFieldV0

Schema: static-collective/recurrence-field/v0

Policy: explicit-repeated-region-testimony/v0

Authority: testimony-only

A recurrence group must contain at least two explicitly admitted occurrences sharing one recurrenceGroupId.

Each occurrence retains:

- occurrenceId;
- recurrenceGroupId;
- exact start/end frames;
- optional confidence;
- label;
- source kind;
- exact source hash;
- method;
- testimony-only authority.

The field binds to the exact ListeningField, song, form hash, fps, and frame domain.

## Hard recurrence gate

017 does not derive recurrence from:

- section labels;
- the word chorus;
- repeated lyric text;
- material returns;
- visual similarity;
- one isolated region.

    ONE OCCURRENCE != RECURRENCE
    SECTION LABEL != RECURRENCE EVIDENCE
    LYRIC REPETITION != RECURRENCE EVIDENCE

With zero admitted recurrence regions, the recurrence field is valid and empty.

## CreativeWeatherV1

016 CreativeWeatherV0 remains untouched and valid.

017 adds a new schema:

static-collective/creative-weather/v1

It composes the exact V0 weather sample with two additional recurrence channels.

### recurrence-presence

Strength exists only while the sample frame lies inside an admitted recurrence occurrence.

Strength is bounded by the occurrence confidence when supplied.

### recurrence-entry

Confidence-weighted linear proximity to the exact start frame of admitted recurrence occurrences.

This creates a bounded arrival front around a known return region.

Both channels retain:

- RecurrenceField hash;
- exact recurrence group IDs;
- exact occurrence IDs;
- method;
- local strength.

## V0 compatibility

CreativeWeatherV0 hashes and semantics do not change.

    NO RECURRENCE FIELD
          →
    NO CREATIVE WEATHER V1

This avoids retroactively turning ordinary 016 ListeningField testimony into recurrence.
